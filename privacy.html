Privacy Policy

Rita Mail Reader is a personal tool used only by its owner to read his own Gmail account. It requests read-only access (gmail.readonly) and cannot send, delete, or modify email.

Email data is processed only on the owner's own computer to produce private summaries for the owner. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
